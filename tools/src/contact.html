Before you write

Common questions.

Plan my safari [[arrow]]
How far in advance should I book?

For July to October and the festive season, six to twelve months ahead is ideal, especially for the Mara River crossings and smaller camps. For other months, three to six months is usually comfortable. Shorter notice is often possible, so it is always worth asking.

How do I pay, and what deposit is needed?

A 30% non-refundable deposit confirms your safari, with the balance due 45 to 60 days before travel. Bookings made within 60 days of travel are paid in full at booking. Payment is by bank transfer or other approved methods, as set out in our terms and conditions.

Can you arrange flights and Zanzibar extensions?

We arrange domestic flights within Tanzania, including to Zanzibar, and beach stays to end your journey. International flights to Tanzania are booked separately, and we are happy to advise on the best arrival airports and times.

Do you work with travel agents?

Yes. Choose "Travel agents & partners" in the form above and tell us about your clients. We will respond with availability and rates.
