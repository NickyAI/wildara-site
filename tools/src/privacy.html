Privacy Policy.

Your trust matters to us as much as your journey. This policy explains what personal information Wildara Africa Safaris collects, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

Wildara Africa Safaris ("Wildara", "we", "us") is a private safari operator based in Arusha, Tanzania. We are responsible for the personal information you share with us through this website, by email, by phone or messaging, and when you travel with us.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use our safari planner, contact form or newsletter sign-up, or when you book, we may collect:

  • your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number and country of residence;
  • travel preferences such as dates, interests, group size, children's ages, accommodation style and budget;
  • booking details including the names of fellow travellers, passport details where required by parks, lodges or airlines, and flight information;
  • health, mobility or dietary information you choose to share so we can care for you properly;
  • payment records (we do not store full card details on this website).

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, for security and performance. This website does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies.

3. How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and prepare tailored safari proposals.
  • To make and manage bookings with lodges, parks, airlines and other suppliers.
  • To look after you before and during your trip, including in an emergency.
  • To send our newsletter, only if you have asked to receive it. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and to protect our rights.
  • To improve our website and services.

4. Legal basis

Where data protection laws require a legal basis, we rely on: taking steps at your request before entering into a contract and performing that contract (enquiries and bookings); your consent (newsletter and sensitive information such as health details); our legitimate interests in running and improving our business; and compliance with legal obligations.

5. Sharing your information

We never sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to deliver your safari or run our business, for example with:

  • lodges, camps, hotels, airlines, transfer providers, national park and conservation authorities;
  • service providers who host our website, deliver email or process enquiry forms on our behalf;
  • professional advisers, insurers, and authorities where required by law or to protect someone's safety.

These parties may only use your information for the purpose we share it for.

6. International transfers

Because we operate in Tanzania and work with international service providers, your information may be processed outside the country where you live. Where we transfer information, we take reasonable steps to ensure it is protected.

7. Cookies & browser storage

This website does not set advertising or analytics cookies. It uses your browser's session storage to remember small preferences, such as whether you have already seen our introductory animation, and this is cleared when you close your browser. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. Embedded services may be added in future, and this policy will be updated if they set cookies.

8. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed to respond and follow up, and booking records for as long as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Newsletter details are kept until you unsubscribe. We then delete or anonymise the information.

9. Keeping it secure

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please avoid sending card or passport details by unencrypted email.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or update it;
  • ask us to delete it, or to restrict or object to how we use it;
  • withdraw consent at any time, for example by unsubscribing from our newsletter;
  • complain to your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We may need to confirm your identity before responding.

11. Children

Our website is intended for adults planning travel. We collect information about children only when a parent or guardian provides it to arrange a family safari.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date it was last updated.

13. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or how we handle your information, please reach us through our contact page. Wildara Africa Safaris, Arusha, Tanzania.

Please also read our Terms & Conditions, which apply to all bookings.
