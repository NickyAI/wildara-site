How Much Does a Tanzania Safari Cost?

The Wildara Team18 September 20266 min read
In this guideWhat drives the priceWhy group size mattersWhat is includedWhat is not includedDeposits and paymentTipping: a guide to typical rangesFAQs

It is one of the first questions every traveller asks, and one of the hardest to answer in a single number. A Tanzania safari can be shaped to many budgets, and the final cost depends on how long you travel, where you go, when you go, where you sleep and how many people share the journey. We believe in clear, honest pricing, so this guide explains what drives the cost of a private safari, shares real examples from our own itineraries and sets out exactly what is and is not included.

What drives the price

Several factors shape the cost of any safari. Park and conservation fees are a significant part of the total, and the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater carry some of the higher fees in the region. Accommodation style matters too, from comfortable lodges to intimate tented camps in remote areas. Season plays its part, with the dry months and festive period in highest demand. Length of trip and route are equally important. A short safari to Tarangire and Lake Manyara involves less driving and lower fees than a long journey to the far north of the Serengeti. Domestic flights, optional activities and the number of travellers sharing the vehicle all add to or reduce the final figure.

Why group size matters

Every Wildara safari is private, with your own vehicle and guide. Because the costs of the vehicle, fuel and guide are shared, the price per person falls as your group grows. Our prices are quoted per person in US dollars, and these examples from our current itineraries show how the tiers work. Prices vary with season, dates and choice of accommodation, but the principle remains the same across every itinerary we offer, from two days to twelve.

  • 2-day Tarangire and Manyara trip: $550 per person for 2 travellers, falling to $400 per person for 7
  • 7-day Big Five safari: $2,500 per person for 2 travellers, falling to $1,670 per person for 7
  • 12-day Mara River migration safari: $4,900 per person for 2 travellers, falling to $3,850 per person for 7

Travelling with friends or family can therefore make a longer, more ambitious safari surprisingly achievable. We are always happy to quote for your exact group size and dates. Even in a larger group, every traveller still enjoys a window seat and a clear view through the pop-up roof, because our Land Cruisers are configured with comfort in mind. Larger groups can also share the cost of optional extras.

What is included

We aim to make our prices as complete as possible, so there are no unwelcome surprises once you arrive. Your safari price covers the essentials of the journey from the moment we meet you. Once you have paid your balance, the core costs of your safari are covered, and you can simply enjoy the journey without reaching for your wallet at every park gate.

  • A private safari with a professional English-speaking guide
  • A 4x4 Toyota Land Cruiser with pop-up roof, binoculars, charging points and mineral water
  • Full board accommodation on safari, with picnic lunches on game drive days
  • Park fees and Ngorongoro Crater fees
  • Airport and hotel transfers
  • Government taxes

What is not included

A few costs fall outside the safari price, either because they depend on personal choice or because they are arranged separately. We will always make these clear in your quotation, so you can budget for them with confidence before you travel. Most guests find these extras modest compared with the overall cost of the safari, and many are entirely optional.

  • International flights
  • Visas
  • Travel insurance, which we consider essential
  • Drinks at lodges
  • Laundry
  • Tips for your guide and lodge staff
  • Personal expenses such as souvenirs

Optional extras such as a hot-air balloon flight, walking safari, bush dinner or sundowner, domestic flights and cultural visits can be added and priced to suit your plans. A dawn balloon flight over the Serengeti or a candlelit dinner under the stars can be the highlight of a trip, and we are happy to advise on which extras suit your route.

Deposits and payment

Once you are happy with your itinerary, a 30% deposit secures your booking. This deposit is non-refundable, because we use it to confirm lodges, camps and arrangements on your behalf, many of which require their own advance payments. The balance is due 45 to 60 days before travel. We will confirm the exact date when you book. Given the deposit terms, we strongly recommend taking out comprehensive travel insurance with cancellation cover at the time of booking, rather than closer to departure.

Tipping: a guide to typical ranges

Tipping is customary on safari in Tanzania and is a meaningful part of income for guides and lodge teams. It is always at your discretion and should reflect the service you received. The figures below are guidance only, reflecting ranges commonly suggested across the industry, and you should feel free to adjust them. There is no obligation, and no one will ask.

  • Safari guide: many travellers give around $20 to $30 per day from their group
  • Lodge and camp staff: often around $10 to $15 per day per group, placed in a communal tip box
  • Transfer drivers and porters: a few dollars per service is typical
  • US dollars or Tanzanian shillings are both welcome; clean, recent notes are best

Frequently asked questions

Are your prices per person or per group?

Our prices are quoted per person in US dollars and depend on the number of travellers. Because every safari is private, costs such as the vehicle and guide are shared within your group, so the per person price falls as the group grows. For example, our 7-day Big Five safari is $2,500 per person for two and $1,670 per person for seven.

Why are park fees such a large part of the cost?

Park and conservation fees fund the protection of Tanzania's wildlife and wild places, including ranger patrols, anti-poaching work and park maintenance. Fees for the Serengeti and the Ngorongoro Crater are among the higher ones in the region. These fees are included in our prices, so you will not need to pay them separately at the gates.

Is the deposit refundable?

The 30% deposit is non-refundable, as it is used to secure accommodation and arrangements on your behalf, many of which require advance payments of their own. The balance is due 45 to 60 days before travel. For this reason we strongly recommend arranging travel insurance with good cancellation cover as soon as you confirm your booking.

How much should I budget for tips?

Tipping is discretionary, so treat any figures as guidance only. Many travellers budget around $20 to $30 per day from their group for their guide, plus around $10 to $15 per day for lodge staff, usually placed in a communal box. Adjust these amounts to reflect your experience, the length of your trip and the size of your group.
