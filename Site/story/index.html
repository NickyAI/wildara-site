Wild Within

Discovery begins in the detail.

We are a private safari company based in Arusha, at the foot of Mount Meru. We exist to help travellers see Tanzania the way we do: slowly, curiously, and with deep respect.

Who we are

Adventurous in spirit. Sophisticated in expression.

Wildara was born from a simple belief: the most powerful moments on safari are rarely the loudest. They are the flick of an ear in the long grass, the hush before a river crossing, the shape of a leopard you only notice because someone taught you how to look.

We design private journeys across northern Tanzania, from the Serengeti and Ngorongoro to Tarangire, Lake Manyara, Lake Eyasi and the shores of Zanzibar. Every trip is shaped around the people taking it, guided by professionals who know these landscapes intimately, and paced to leave room for wonder.

We call it Wild Within. Because the wild is not only something you travel to see. It is something a great journey awakens in you.

The meaning of our W

Look closer. The wild is hiding in plain sight.

Our wordmark combines refined lettering with a subtle wildlife silhouette hidden within the W. Most people see a letter first. Look again and a fox appears, and an elephant’s trunk. It is our promise in a single mark: there is always more to discover.

The fox

Curiosity and observation. The patient art of noticing what others pass by, and the guides who teach you to see it.

The trunk

Strength, memory and connection. Journeys built on care, local knowledge and relationships that last beyond a single trip.

The flow

Movement across an endless land. Itineraries that follow the seasons, the herds and the light rather than a rigid timetable.

The detail

Discovery begins in the detail. From the first conversation to your welcome at the airport, nothing is left to chance.

How we travel

What you can count on.

The same standards apply whether you join us for two days in Tarangire or twelve days following the Great Migration.

i.

Always private

Your own vehicle and guide, your own pace. Linger at a sighting for an hour or leave early for a quiet sundowner. The day bends around you.

ii.

Professional guides

English-speaking guides who track, interpret and host with warmth. They know when to share a story and when to switch off the engine and simply wait.

iii.

Built for the bush

4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers with pop-up roofs for a 360° view, plus binoculars, charging points and mineral water always on board.

iv.

Thoughtful stays

Lodges and tented camps chosen for their setting, comfort and service, from Karatu’s green highlands to camps in the heart of the Serengeti.

v.

Honest pricing

Park fees, crater fees, transfers, taxes and game drives are included in every quote. What is not included is clearly listed before you book.

vi.

Respect for the wild

We keep a respectful distance from wildlife, stay on permitted tracks and ask before photographing people. The places we love should thrive long after we leave.

About us

Getting to know us.

Contact us
Where is Wildara Africa Safaris based?

We are based in Arusha, the safari capital of northern Tanzania. It is the natural starting point for the Serengeti, Ngorongoro Crater, Tarangire and Lake Manyara, and only about an hour from Kilimanjaro International Airport.

Do you only run private safaris?

Yes. Every journey is private, so you never share your vehicle or guide with strangers. That means a flexible pace, more time at sightings and an itinerary that can change as the wild unfolds.

Can you create a completely custom itinerary?

Absolutely. Our published safaris are starting points. We can combine parks, add experiences such as ballooning or cultural visits, adjust accommodation and extend your trip to Zanzibar. Tell us your dates and wishes and we will design the route.

How quickly will you reply to my enquiry?

We usually reply within one working day with ideas, questions or a first proposal. Our team works on East Africa Time (UTC+3).
