Honeymoons and Romance

Golden plains by day, turquoise waters to follow: a journey made for two.

Add to my safari Matching itineraries
The experience

Few journeys suit a honeymoon quite like a safari followed by days on the beach. The combination of adventure and tranquillity creates a rhythm that feels both exciting and deeply restorative. On safari, you share early mornings watching the plains come alive, afternoons following elephants and lions, and evenings toasting the sunset. Then, on Zanzibar, the pace softens into long, lazy days by the Indian Ocean, with nothing to plan beyond the next swim.

A private safari is inherently romantic. With your own vehicle and guide, there is space to linger, talk and enjoy the experience together without distraction. We can add touches that make it unmistakably yours: a private bush dinner by lantern light, a sundowner with champagne, a hot-air balloon flight at dawn or a celebration cake at the lodge. Each is available on request, so the honeymoon unfolds exactly as you imagine it.

Zanzibar completes the journey beautifully. The island blends Swahili, Arab, Indian and European influences, and its historic centre, Stone Town, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site of winding lanes, carved doors and fragrant spice markets. In the north, Nungwi offers lively beaches and dhow sunset cruises, while Matemwe on the northeast coast is quieter, with a lovely sense of seclusion. Domestic flights from the safari circuit can be arranged on request.

How it unfolds

Moment by moment.

Add to my safari

Sunrise together

The first morning of your honeymoon safari starts with coffee and the soft glow of dawn. You stand side by side in the open roof of the Land Cruiser as the Serengeti stretches out before you, sharing the quiet thrill of seeing a lioness move through the golden grass.

A table for two

One evening, your guide drives you to a secluded spot. Lanterns flicker, a table is set, and a fire glows beneath the stars. There is champagne, a candlelit meal and the sounds of the African night all around. It feels like the world has narrowed to just the two of you.

Island time

A short flight takes you to Zanzibar. Suddenly the dust of the plains gives way to white sand and water in every shade of turquoise. Days are spent swimming, reading, wandering the beach and drifting into the slow, relaxed rhythm of island life.

Sunset on a dhow

On your final evening, you board a traditional wooden dhow as the sun lowers over the Indian Ocean. The sail fills, the sky blushes pink and gold, and you raise a glass to the journey. It is a fitting close to a honeymoon you will talk about for years.

FAQs

Before you go.

Plan my safari Ask a question
Why combine safari with Zanzibar?

Safari days are thrilling but often begin early and involve plenty of time on game drives. Ending your trip on Zanzibar offers the perfect balance: time to rest, swim and reflect on the experience together. The island's culture, history and beaches add a completely different flavour to the journey, making it feel like two holidays in one.

How long should a honeymoon safari be?

We suggest around five to seven days on safari, followed by three to five nights on Zanzibar. This gives you time to explore the Serengeti and Ngorongoro Crater without rushing, before unwinding by the sea. Shorter or longer journeys are certainly possible, and we will tailor the balance between bush and beach to suit your preferences.

Which part of Zanzibar is best for honeymooners?

It depends on the mood you are looking for. Nungwi, on the northern tip, has lively beaches, beautiful sunsets and plenty of restaurants. Matemwe, on the northeast coast, is quieter and more secluded. Many couples also spend a night or two in Stone Town to explore its history and atmosphere. We will help you find the right fit.

Can you arrange special honeymoon touches?

Yes. On request, we can arrange a private bush dinner or sundowner, a hot-air balloon flight over the Serengeti, a celebration cake, wine or champagne. We also share your honeymoon details with lodges, many of which enjoy adding small touches of their own. Tell us what would make the trip special for you, and we will plan around it.

When is the best time for a honeymoon safari?

Tanzania can be visited year-round. The long dry season, roughly June to October, is popular for wildlife viewing and coincides with the Mara River crossings. The months from January to March bring the calving season in the southern Serengeti and generally good beach weather on Zanzibar. We will help you choose dates around your wedding.
