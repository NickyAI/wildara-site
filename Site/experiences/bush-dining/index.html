Bush Dining and Sundowners

Lantern light, the scent of woodsmoke and a table set beneath African stars.

Add to my safari Matching itineraries
The experience

On safari, food is never just about the plate. It is about where you sit, what you hear and the light that falls around you. Every Wildara safari is full board, and on game drive days lunch is a picnic enjoyed somewhere scenic in the park: beneath a spreading tree, on a rise overlooking the plains or beside a quiet waterhole. These simple meals in wild places often become cherished memories.

The sundowner is perhaps the most beloved of safari rituals. As the afternoon heat fades, your guide finds a spot with a view, and a drink is poured as the sky turns from gold to crimson to violet. The sounds of the bush shift as night approaches. Elephants move towards water, birds settle, and somewhere in the distance a lion may begin to call. It is a moment to pause and simply take it all in.

For something more special, a private bush dinner or sundowner can be arranged on request as a supplement, depending on lodge and park guidelines. Picture a table set beneath the stars, lanterns glowing, a fire crackling nearby, and a meal prepared and served in the open. It is an ideal way to mark a birthday, anniversary or honeymoon, and we can add a cake, wine or champagne to make the evening complete.

Moment by moment.

The picnic

Midway through a long game drive, your guide pulls into the shade of a large acacia. Lunch boxes are opened, cold drinks passed round, and you eat looking out over the savannah. A family of giraffes browses nearby, and the hum of insects fills the warm afternoon air.

Golden hour

As the sun drops lower, the light softens and the plains glow. Your guide finds a vantage point, and you stretch your legs beside the vehicle with a drink in hand. Silhouettes of trees stand sharp against the sky. Conversation slows; everyone turns to watch the sun sink.

Lantern light

After dark, a short walk or drive brings you to a clearing transformed by lanterns. A table is set with care, a fire crackles, and the night sounds of the bush surround you. Dinner is served course by course under a sky crowded with stars.

A toast to the day

Glasses are raised to the animals you have seen and the people you share them with. Perhaps there is a birthday cake, perhaps a bottle of champagne. Later, back at your room, you fall asleep to the calls of the night, already sure you will remember this evening for years.

FAQs

Before you go.

Plan my safari Ask a question
Are meals included on safari?

Yes. Every Wildara safari is full board, which means breakfast, lunch and dinner are included during the safari. On game drive days, lunch is usually a picnic enjoyed in a scenic spot inside the park, allowing you to make the most of the day without returning to the lodge. Mineral water is available on board throughout your game drives.

What is a sundowner?

A sundowner is a cherished safari tradition: a drink enjoyed at sunset, usually at a scenic spot with wide views over the bush. It is a chance to stretch your legs, reflect on the day and watch the colours of the sky change as night approaches. Simple sundowners are a natural part of many safaris, and a special set-up can be arranged on request.

How does a private bush dinner work?

A bush dinner is arranged with your lodge or camp, depending on its facilities and park guidelines. A table is set in a scenic location nearby, often lit by lanterns and a campfire, and dinner is served outdoors. It is available as an optional supplement on request. Let us know your preferred evening when planning, so we can confirm availability in advance.

Can you cater for dietary requirements?

Lodges and camps in northern Tanzania are generally experienced in catering for vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free and other dietary needs. Please share any requirements or allergies with us when booking, and we will pass them on to each property and to your guide for picnic lunches. Advance notice helps ensure everything runs smoothly in remote locations.

Can we celebrate a special occasion?

Absolutely. Birthdays, anniversaries and honeymoons are wonderful to celebrate on safari. We can arrange a cake, wine or champagne on request, and a private bush dinner or sundowner makes a memorable centrepiece. Tell us about the occasion when you enquire, and we will help you plan the details so the moment feels effortless.
