Family Safaris

Shared discoveries, wide eyes and stories your family will retell for years.

The experience

There is something special about seeing Africa through a child's eyes. The first elephant, close enough to hear it breathing. A giraffe peering over a treetop. A lion cub tumbling in the grass. Children notice details adults miss, from a chameleon on a branch to a dung beetle at work, and their excitement is wonderfully contagious. A family safari is an adventure that brings generations together, creating shared memories that last long after you return home.

A private safari is ideally suited to families. With your own vehicle and guide, the pace is entirely yours. Game drives can be shorter when attention spans run low, breaks can be taken whenever needed, and there is no worry about disturbing other guests. The pop-up roof gives everyone a clear view, binoculars are on board for budding naturalists, and charging points keep cameras and tablets ready for every sighting. Mineral water on board keeps everyone refreshed between stops.

We design family itineraries with variety and comfort in mind. Northern Tanzania works beautifully, with relatively short drives between Tarangire, Lake Manyara, the Ngorongoro Crater and the Serengeti, and each park offers something different. Cultural visits, walks where permitted and time to relax at the lodge help balance the days. Many families finish with a few nights on Zanzibar's beaches, giving everyone time to unwind together after the excitement of the safari.

The first sighting

Within the first hour of your first game drive, a small voice shouts from the back seat. There, by the road, stands an elephant. Everyone scrambles for the pop-up roof. Binoculars are passed around, questions tumble out, and your guide patiently answers each one with a smile.

Learning in the wild

Your guide turns the bush into a classroom without walls. Children learn to identify tracks, tell a zebra's stripes apart and spot birds by their colours. Some keep a sightings list; others sketch what they see. Every drive becomes a treasure hunt, with new discoveries around each bend.

Picnic and play

Lunch is a picnic in the park, eaten in the shade with animals in the distance. Afterwards, there is time to rest at the lodge, swim in the pool or simply share stories of the morning. The pace allows everyone, from youngest to oldest, to enjoy each day.

Evening tales

As the day ends, families gather to relive the highlights. The children argue cheerfully over the best sighting of the day. Perhaps there is a campfire, a sky full of stars and the distant call of a hyena. Bedtime comes easily after so much adventure.

FAQs

Before you go.

Plan my safari Ask a question
What age is best for a family safari?

There is no single right age, and families travel with children of all ages. Many find that from around six years old, children have the patience and curiosity to fully enjoy game drives. Younger children can certainly travel too, especially on a private safari where the pace is flexible. Some activities have their own age guidelines, which we will explain when planning.

Is a private safari better for families?

We believe so. With your own vehicle and guide, you set the pace, take breaks when needed and adapt the day to your children's energy levels. There is no pressure to stay quiet for the sake of other guests, and your guide can tailor explanations to younger listeners. Every Wildara safari is private as standard.

How long are the drives between parks?

Northern Tanzania is relatively compact. The drive from Arusha to Tarangire typically takes around two hours, and transfers between Lake Manyara, Karatu and the Ngorongoro Crater are fairly short. The journey into the Serengeti is longer, but it includes game viewing along the way. We plan family itineraries to keep driving days comfortable.

Are family safaris safe?

Safety is central to every safari we arrange. Game drives take place within the vehicle, with an experienced guide who knows the parks and animal behaviour well. Lodges are chosen with families in mind. As with any travel, sensible precautions apply, such as following your guide's advice and preparing for the sun and insects.

Can we combine a family safari with a beach holiday?

Yes, and it is a very popular choice. After the excitement of the safari, a few days on Zanzibar give everyone time to relax. The island offers warm water, sandy beaches and the historic streets of Stone Town to explore. Domestic flights from the northern circuit to Zanzibar can be arranged on request.
