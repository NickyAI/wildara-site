Overview

A dream for African Adventure!

This 2-day safari offers a wonderful opportunity to discover Tanzania’s wild beauty, encounter incredible wildlife and enjoy the breathtaking landscapes of Africa. Every moment brings you closer to the magic of the wilderness.

With Wildara Africa Safaris, enjoy a private safari with a professional English-speaking guide, full-board accommodation and a comfortable 4x4 safari vehicle equipped with mineral water, binoculars and charging points, all complemented by warm local hospitality and carefully selected accommodations.

    Day 1 · Arusha – Tarangire · 2 hours

    Lake Manyara National Park

    Begin this day with an early pick-up from your lodge or directly from airport and starts beautiful journey toward Tarangire National Park while carrying our packed lunch, you’ll dive straight into the adventure with exciting game drives through stunning landscapes filled with wildlife and iconic baobab trees. Later in the afternoon, we transfer to Karatu for a delicious dinner and overnight stay.

    Kankari Lodge - Mid range
    2
    Day 2 · Ngorongoro – Arusha · 3 hours.

    Ngorongoro Crater - End of Itinerary

    After a hearty breakfast, descend into the breathtaking Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable day of game drives. Explore its lush plains, crater floor, and diverse habitats, where a stunning variety of wildlife awaits. After your adventure inside the crater, we start the scenic drive back to Arusha or Kilimanjaro Airport for your onward journey.

        Is this a private safari?

        Yes. Every Wildara safari is private, so the vehicle, the guide and the pace are yours alone. You travel in a 4x4 Toyota Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof, binoculars, charging points and mineral water on board, with a professional English-speaking guide throughout.

        How much does the 2-day safari cost?

        Prices are per person in USD and depend on group size: $650 per person for 2 travellers, falling to $480 per person for a group of 6. Larger groups are quoted on request. Prices may change before full payment, as set out in our terms and conditions.

        When is the best time to do this safari?

        The parks on this route are rewarding all year. The dry season from June to October brings easy game viewing around water, while the green season from November to May offers lush landscapes, newborn animals, quieter parks and often better lodge availability. We will suggest the best dates for what you want to see.

        What is included in the price?

        Airport transfers, a private 4x4 safari vehicle and guide, all park entrance and conservation fees, the Ngorongoro Crater service fee, all game drives listed, accommodation on the stated meal basis, picnic lunches on game drives, drinking water and government taxes. International flights, visas, travel insurance, tips and drinks at lodges are not included.

        Where does the safari start and finish?

        Your guide meets you at Kilimanjaro International Airport or Arusha Airport on day 1 and transfers you to your hotel. On the final day we drop you at the airport about three hours before your international flight.

        Where will we stay?

        This itinerary uses Kankari Lodge. Each is chosen for its location, comfort and warm service. If you would like to upgrade to a more exclusive camp or change the style of accommodation, we will happily adjust the proposal.

        Can we change or extend the itinerary?

        Of course. Every itinerary is a starting point. We can add nights, swap parks, include a hot-air balloon flight or walking safari, or add a few days on Zanzibar. Tell us what matters to you and we will reshape the route and the price.

        How do we book, and what deposit is needed?

        Send an enquiry and we will reply with a tailored proposal. A 30% non-refundable deposit confirms your booking, and the balance is due 45 to 60 days before travel. Bookings made within 60 days of travel are paid in full at booking.
